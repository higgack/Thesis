**Alternative-exclusion sensitivity analysis.** To examine indirectly the sensitivity related to the IIA assumption, a binary logit of integrated versus bonding-only was estimated on the sample excluding the process-only type (N = 763). The odds ratio was 1.463 for technological scope and 0.987 for filing year, so the direction and relative magnitude of the main coefficients were similar to the values in the integrated-type equation of the multinomial logit (1.403 and 0.993). This sensitivity analysis shows that excluding the process-only type does not substantially change the interpretation of the results for the integrated type; it does not, however, replace formal IIA tests (Hausman–McFadden, Small–Hsiao). The core technology model in the main text also uses the same sample.

### Appendix B. Irrelevant uses of the search terms

The search terms of the keyword filter are also used in senses unrelated to hybrid bonding. Most such cases are uses of *direct bonding* in a different sense, and a few are uses of *hybrid bonding* in the sense of chemical bonding or bonding sheets for power modules. Applications whose titles contained the following terms were judged to be irrelevant uses: ceramic, DBC (direct bonded copper), alumina, zirconia, and aluminum nitride substrates; copper alloys; diamond–molybdenum bonding; laser precursors; electrostatic chucks; power module and power device substrates; heat spreaders; direct bonded metal substrates; direct bonded circuit assemblies; and sp³–sp² hybrid bonding (in the sense of chemical bonding). These applications number 68 (63 for *direct bonding* and 5 for *hybrid bonding*), and their distribution is shown in Table B1. After the irrelevant uses are excluded, many of the remaining applications filed before 2010 concern the direct bonding of silicon wafers (e.g., for manufacturing silicon-on-insulator (SOI) wafers); because these are precursor technologies of the dielectric bonding in hybrid bonding, they were retained in the sample. The list of the 68 titles is available from the authors upon request.
